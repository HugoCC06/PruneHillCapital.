Equity Analyst / Portfolio Manager

Applications open

Research companies, build models, write investment memos and pitch ideas to the Investment Committee.

Analysts support the Head of Research and Portfolio Managers with company research, financial modelling, valuation, industry analysis and monitoring. Portfolio Managers own sector or geography coverage, present trade pitches and monitor their areas.

What you'll get

  • Real decisions on a real portfolio, inside a professional mandate
  • Training in valuation, modelling and investment writing
  • Experience pitching to and voting on an Investment Committee
  • An ESG and risk management grounding employers look for
  • Concrete examples for internship and graduate applications
  • A network across Royal Holloway and other UK student funds

FAQ

Questions applicants ask.

Can't find your answer? Get in touch.

Q01

Do I need to study finance or economics?

No. The Fund is open to every enrolled RHUL student, whatever their degree. We care about curiosity, rigour and commitment.

Q02

How much time does it take?

Around 6-8 hours a week during term, including bi-weekly research meetings on campus (with an online option).

Q03

Is it real money?

Yes. The Fund manages a real portfolio through a brokerage account held under RHUL's institutional framework, with the Academic Adviser as authorised signatory.

Q04

Do members get paid or share in profits?

No. The Fund is strictly non-profit. Once at scale, up to 30% of realised net profits may go to RHUL hardship and access support.

Q05

What do the interviews involve?

A get-to-know conversation, then a second interview with five practical questions. Clear reasoning matters more than technical knowledge.

Q06

Which year groups can apply?

All year groups, undergraduate and postgraduate, as long as you're an enrolled Royal Holloway student.

Q07

What can't the Fund invest in?

Derivatives, leverage, crypto, unlisted securities, fossil fuels, armaments, gambling, adult entertainment and tobacco. See the full rules.
