Process & governance

Every decision has a process and an owner.

From the first research note to the trade blotter, here's how ideas become positions and how the Fund stays accountable.

Trade process · s5.2

From idea to execution.

STEP 01

Research

Ideas are developed with written analysis, coordinated by the Head of Research, who sets research priorities and coverage.

Owner: PMs and Analysts
STEP 02

Memo

A written investment memo covering thesis, valuation, ESG factors, risks and expected return.

Owner: Portfolio Manager
STEP 03

Pitch

The PM presents the idea to the Investment Committee and defends it under questioning.

Owner: Portfolio Manager
STEP 04

Vote

The Committee discusses, votes, and approves or rejects the trade.

Owner: Investment Committee
STEP 05

Execute

Checked against risk limits, executed and recorded in the trade blotter and minutes.

Owner: Risk & Compliance, Operations
The investment memo

What every pitch must cover.

Pitches are judged on the quality of the thinking, not the confidence of the presenter. Each one needs written analysis on five points.

Check your trade against the mandate first

SectionWhat the Committee looks for
Investment thesisWhy this, why now, and what the market is missing.
ValuationA clear view of what it's worth and the assumptions behind it.
ESG factorsExclusion screening and material sustainability considerations.
RisksWhat could go wrong, how likely it is and how it fits portfolio limits.
Expected returnThe upside, the time frame and the position sizing proposed.
Meeting cadence · s5.1

A rhythm built for students.

Bi-weeklyResearch meetings

In person on the RHUL campus when possible, with an online option available.

QuarterlyPortfolio review

Formal in-person review of performance, rebalancing needs and mandate compliance.

AnnualMandate review

Full review of the mandate, benchmarks, risk limits and Advisory Board input, with the Academic Adviser and at least one Advisory Board member.

Governance · s10

Accountability built in.

Committee decisions

All investment decisions are made through the Investment Committee, in line with the approved governance framework.

Pre-trade checks

The Risk & Compliance Officer checks every proposed trade against the mandate before execution.

Breach handling

Breaches are reported, corrected promptly, and documented in minutes and reports.

Drawdown review

A 15% drawdown over six months or 20% over twelve months triggers a formal investment and leadership review.

Broker oversight

The Academic Adviser is the authorised signatory and routes all account-level broker communications.

Mandate amendments

The mandate is reviewed at least annually. Any amendment needs written approval from the Academic Adviser.

Reporting

Transparent by default.

The Operations & Reporting Officer manages reporting and document control across three regular outputs.

MonthlyFactsheet

Holdings, allocation and performance against benchmarks.

QuarterlyPortfolio review

Performance, rebalancing and compliance with the mandate.

AnnuallyAnnual report

A full account of the year, reviewed alongside the mandate.

Recruiting now

Open to every enrolled Royal Holloway student. No finance background needed, just curiosity, rigour and around 6-8 hours a week in term.
