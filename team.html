The people behind the portfolio.

Leadership & oversight

Leadership team.

HC
Chief Investment Officer

Hugo Christie

Coordinates the investment process and chairs the Investment Committee.

  • Point of contact for the Academic Adviser, broker and Advisory Board
  • Works with the officers and Head of Research to keep the process running
  • Helps new members find their feet
JN
Academic Adviser

Julinda Nuri

Provides academic oversight of the Fund on behalf of the Business School.

  • Authorised signatory for the brokerage account
  • Routes all account-level communications with the broker
  • Approves mandate amendments, benchmark changes and partnerships
MS
Head of Research

Manjit Singh

Leads the research function and coordinates the Portfolio Managers and Analysts.

  • Oversees research standards and investment theses
  • Allocates research coverage
  • Runs the research pipeline
C
Risk & Compliance Officer

Christa

Keeps every decision inside the mandate.

  • Monitors mandate adherence
  • Maintains the risk register
  • Checks all trades before execution
VS
Operations & Reporting Officer

Veer Shinh

Runs the Fund's operations and reporting.

  • Manages reporting and factsheets
  • Document control
  • Operational workflows
+
Advisory Board

3+ members

Individuals with finance or academic expertise who give non-executive guidance on strategy, governance and long-term development.

  • Two-year terms
  • Attend at least one annual review meeting each year

Investment Committee

Where decisions are made.

All investment decisions go through the Investment Committee. It discusses each pitch, votes and approves trades.

How the committee process works

MemberRole on the Committee
CIOChairs the Committee
Academic AdviserOversight; approves benchmark and mandate changes

Portfolio ManagersPitch ideas and vote
